    The Code of the Elite

    • Headshots pay extra and kill instantly — even the toughest outlaws.
    • A red ring means an outlaw is drawing a bead on you. Shoot first or take cover.
    • Bullets drop over distance and drift in the wind. Use the scope's range ticks and the wind flag.
    • Hold Space to steady your breath. Hold it too long and you'll gasp.
    • Dead-Eye fills with every kill. Press F to slow time and steady your hand.
    • Lead your shots on riders and the train.
    • Red powder barrels explode. Civilians do not — don't shoot them.
    • Earn stars for accuracy and headshots to open new territories.
